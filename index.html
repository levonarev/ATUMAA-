Wake the grove before night falls

  1. Drift into glowing motes. They trail behind you in the order you gather them.
  2. Each standing stone asks for signs in sequence. The mote nearest you is always offered first.
  3. Wrong mote in front? Release it, then gather it again to send it to the back.
  4. Shades scatter your trail and steal light. A waking stone banishes any shade close by.
